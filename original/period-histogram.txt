The comb is the whole story of this picture. Even lengths are common and
odd lengths are rare. Nothing about the algorithm suggests that.


2. WHY THE COMB

  odd period    11486   11.5%
  even period   88197   88.5%

An odd period means the first pass lands on -1, so x*x - n*y*y = -1 has a
solution, so -1 is a norm from the field. If any prime factor of n is 3 mod 4
that is impossible.

  CLAIM   L(n) is odd  =>  no prime factor of n is 3 mod 4
  checked for every non-square below 200000, no violations

Roughly three quarters of all numbers have a prime factor 3 mod 4, and that
proportion creeps up. So the odd column keeps thinning.

         M   odd periods among n < M
      1000   15.70%
     10000   13.35%
    100000   11.52%

Dividing by sqrt(n) removes the comb and leaves a smooth decay with a hard
looking right hand edge. The edge is not hard. It drifts.

         M   largest L(n)/sqrt(n) for n < M
      1000   1.9792
     10000   2.2245
    100000   2.4932

That slow drift is the log factor in the known bound L(n) = O(sqrt(n) log n).


4. HOW THE MEAN GROWS

         M     mean L     exponent
      3000      17.06             
     10000      28.38        0.422
     30000      45.94        0.439
    100000      77.84        0.438

The exponent settles near 0.44, not 0.5. The mean period grows a little
slower than the square root. The gap is the class number, which divides the
regulator and is usually bigger than one.
